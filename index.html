Your Visit

How it works

Walk in, sit down, walk out looking sharp. No appointments needed.

Step 01
Walk In
No appointment needed. Walk into Towne Center At Aquia, Suite 111. Grab a seat and you will be up in no time.
Step 02
Consultation
Tell your barber what you want, or show a picture. They will assess your hair and recommend the best approach.
Step 03
The Cut
Precision cutting with clippers and scissors. Your barber takes the time to get every detail right.
Step 04
The Treatment
Hot towel on the face and neck. Warm shaving lotion. Straight razor clean-up around the edges. Back and neck massage.
Step 05
Walk Out Sharp
Final check in the mirror, dust-off, and you are out the door feeling like a million bucks. See you next time.
