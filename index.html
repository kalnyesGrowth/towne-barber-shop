Haircare

How Often Should You Get a Haircut?

For most men, every 3 to 4 weeks keeps a clean look. Military personnel and anyone with a skin fade should come in every 2 weeks to maintain crisp edges. If you are growing it out, 6 weeks is the max before shape is lost. Your barber can set the right schedule based on your hair type and style.

Grooming

Why a Hot Towel Shave Beats Your Razor at Home

A straight razor glides closer than any multi-blade cartridge. The hot towel opens pores and softens the hair, so the blade cuts clean without irritation. Warm lotion soothes and protects the skin after. The result is smoother skin that lasts days longer than your bathroom mirror routine.

Kids

First Haircut Tips: Getting Your Child Ready

Bring your child during a quieter time, usually mid-morning on a weekday. Let them watch another kid get a cut first if possible. Bring their favorite toy or tablet. Our barbers are trained to work with fidgety toddlers, and we keep toys and cartoons on hand. Most kids end up loving it.
