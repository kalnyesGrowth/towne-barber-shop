Our Story

More than a haircut

At Towne Barber Shop, every haircut comes with the full treatment. Hot towel, straight razor neck shave, warm shaving lotion, and a back massage. No upsells, no extras. That is just how we do things.

Located inside Towne Center At Aquia, we serve families, professionals, and military personnel from nearby Quantico. Our barbers remember your name, your cut, and your preferences. Clients keep coming back for years because the service never changes.

With a 4.4-star rating and over 112 verified Google reviews, our work speaks for itself. Walk in any day of the week and see for yourself.

Your Visit

How it works

Walk in, sit down, walk out looking sharp. No appointments needed.

Step 01
Walk In
No appointment needed. Walk into Towne Center At Aquia, Suite 111. Grab a seat and you will be up in no time.
Step 02
Consultation
Tell your barber what you want, or show a picture. They will assess your hair and recommend the best approach.
Step 03
The Cut
Precision cutting with clippers and scissors. Your barber takes the time to get every detail right.
Step 04
The Treatment
Hot towel on the face and neck. Warm shaving lotion. Straight razor clean-up around the edges. Back and neck massage.
Step 05
Walk Out Sharp
Final check in the mirror, dust-off, and you are out the door feeling like a million bucks. See you next time.
